About the job
Job Overview

We are looking for a highly skilled Artificial Intelligence (AI) Specialist to develop, implement, and optimize AI-based solutions. The ideal candidate should have expertise in machine learning, natural language processing, and deep learning frameworks. This position is open for both part-time and full-time professionals.

Responsibilities

Design, develop, and deploy AI models and algorithms.
Work on machine learning, deep learning, and NLP-based projects.
Preprocess and analyze large datasets for training and testing AI models.
Optimize AI models for accuracy, efficiency, and scalability.
Collaborate with software developers, data scientists, and product teams.
Integrate AI solutions into applications, platforms, or business processes.
Research and stay updated on emerging AI trends, tools, and techniques.
Ensure AI solutions are ethical, transparent, and aligned with company goals.

Requirements

Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, AI, or a related field.
Experience in Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras, or Scikit-learn.
Strong knowledge of machine learning, deep learning, and NLP techniques.
Experience working with large datasets and cloud AI services (AWS, Azure, GCP).
Familiarity with AI ethics and responsible AI principles.
Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
Ability to work independently for part-time roles and collaboratively for full-time positions.
