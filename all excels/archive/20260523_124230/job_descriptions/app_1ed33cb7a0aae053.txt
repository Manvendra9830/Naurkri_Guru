About the job
Company Description
Delta International Institute, authorized by the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) under the Government of Dubai, specializes in guiding individuals to achieve consistent success in financial market trading. Through personalized one-on-one sessions and both online and offline mentorship programs, the institute empowers students to generate passive income and develop sustainable trading skills. Committed to fostering a supportive community, Delta International Institute ensures easy access to high-quality financial education and practical market insights.

JOB OVERVIEW 
We are looking for a dynamic and knowledgeable AI Developer/ Mentor who can create engaging content 
and guide learners in the field of Artificial Intelligence, coding, and emerging technologies. The ideal 
candidate should be passionate about teaching, simplifying complex concepts, and staying updated with 
the latest AI tools and trends. 

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES 
● Content Creation: Create and present engaging video content related to AI, coding, and technology. 
● Mentorship: Mentor and support students throughout their learning journey. 
● Concept Simplification: Simplify and explain complex technical concepts in an easy-to-understand 
manner. 
● Live Engagement: Conduct live sessions, workshops, and interactive Q&A sessions. 
● Trend Awareness: Stay updated with the latest trends in AI, tools, and development practices. 
● Curriculum Support: Assist in developing course materials, learning resources, and structured content. 
 REQUIRED SKILLS & QUALIFICATIONS 
● Strong communication and presentation skills. 
● Good understanding of Artificial Intelligence concepts. 
● Hands-on experience with AI tools, specifically Claude and Claude Code. 
● Familiarity with other AI tools used in web and app development.

● Strong problem-solving mindset. 

 PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
