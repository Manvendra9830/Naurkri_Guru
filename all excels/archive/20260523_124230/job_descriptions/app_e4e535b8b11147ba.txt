AI Prompt Engineer – Job Description

About the Role


We are looking for a creative and analytical AI Prompt Engineer who can design, test, and optimize prompts for AI models to generate accurate, useful, and high-quality outputs. This role is ideal for individuals interested in AI, content strategy, and problem-solving.

Key Responsibilities


Design and refine prompts for AI tools (ChatGPT, LLMs, generative AI platforms)
Test and evaluate AI-generated outputs for quality and accuracy
Optimize prompts to improve performance and consistency
Collaborate with teams to understand use cases and requirements
Document prompt strategies and best practices
Analyze AI responses and continuously improve results
Stay updated with latest trends in AI and prompt engineering

Required Skills & Qualifications


Bachelor’s degree in any field (Tech, English, Business preferred)
Strong communication and writing skills
Analytical thinking and problem-solving ability
Basic understanding of AI tools and Large Language Models (LLMs)
Creativity in framing questions and instructions
Attention to detail and experimentation mindset

Preferred Skills (Bonus)


Experience using AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, etc.)
Basic knowledge of Python or scripting
Understanding of NLP (Natural Language Processing)
Experience in content writing, copywriting, or data annotation
Familiarity with prompt optimization techniques

Fast-growing career opportunities in AI domain
