About the job

Location: Remote
Type: Full-Time Internship

• Work on real-world problem statements using data science approaches
• Design and implement data-driven solutions for business use cases
• Build prototypes for predictive and analytical models
• Explore datasets to discover hidden patterns and insights
• Apply statistical techniques to validate hypotheses
• Work on data pipelines and automate data processing tasks
• Collaborate with teams to convert business challenges into analytical problems
• Optimize existing models and improve their efficiency
• Present findings and recommendations in a structured manner

Required Skills

• Understanding of data science concepts and workflows
• Strong proficiency in Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn)
• Knowledge of statistics and probability fundamentals
• Familiarity with machine learning techniques
• Basic understanding of data preprocessing and feature engineering
• Exposure to data visualization tools or libraries
• Problem-solving mindset and analytical thinking
• Ability to communicate insights effectively

Eligibility Criteria

• Students or recent graduates from Data Science, AI, Computer Science, or related fields
• Freshers with strong analytical mindset are encouraged to apply
• Candidates with projects, internships, or case studies preferred
• Certifications in Data Science / Machine Learning are a plus
• Must be available for a full-time remote internship

Perks and Benefits

• Monthly stipend of ₹21,000
• Hands-on experience solving real-world data problems
• Internship completion certificate
• Opportunity to work on innovative data-driven projects
• Exposure to modern tools and workflows

Why Join Acrotinel?

This internship is ideal for individuals who want to gain practical experience in data science, work on impactful projects, and build a strong foundation in analytics and machine learning.
