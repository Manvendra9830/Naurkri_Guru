About TEONOX
TEONOX is a next-generation digital skills training institute focused on building industry-ready professionals. We believe in practical learning, real business exposure, and skill development - not textbook theory.
Running A2 Digital — our digital marketing company — we hired hundreds of candidates. Great degrees. Impressive resumes. But when it came to actual work, most simply weren't ready. And the businesses we worked with weren't struggling because of bad budgets or weak strategies. They were struggling because they couldn't find the right people.
Founded by Amit Manglani — 15+ years in business growth and digital marketing — Teonox is built to bridge the gap between what institutes teach and what the real world demands.

Role Overview
AI is not the future anymore — it's today. Businesses, creators, marketers, and entrepreneurs are all using AI tools right now. But most people don't know how to use them strategically.
That's where you come in.
As Teonox's AI and Data Science Trainer, you won't be teaching coding or complex algorithms. You will teach people how to actually use AI and Data Science in their daily work — to save time, create better content, make smarter decisions, and grow faster.
