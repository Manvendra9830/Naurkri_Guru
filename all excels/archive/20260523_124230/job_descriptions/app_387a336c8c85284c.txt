About the job

Our services enable clients to scale their teams efficiently, manage costs with fixed-price packages, and deliver projects on time.  Role Description
 
This is a full-time remote role for an Entry Level Software Engineer. The Software Engineer will engage in software development, programming, and troubleshooting tasks. Day-to-day responsibilities include contributing to back-end web development projects, designing and implementing software solutions, collaborating with the team to enhance system functionality, and maintaining high code quality. The role also involves applying computer science principles and object-oriented programming techniques to deliver efficient and scalable solutions.

 Qualifications
 
Strong foundation in Computer Science and Software Development
Experience or training in Back-End Web Development and related technologies
Proficiency in Programming and Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)
Ability to solve problems independently and collaboratively within a software engineering team
Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field
Excellent analytical, communication, and organizational skills
Prior experience or familiarity with building automation systems is a plus
