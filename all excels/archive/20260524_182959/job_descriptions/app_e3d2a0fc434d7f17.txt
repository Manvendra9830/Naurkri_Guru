About the job
We are seeking an experienced AI/ML Engineer with expertise in Machine Learning, Generative AI, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems. The ideal candidate should have strong hands-on experience in Python, SQL, end-to-end ML pipelines, and handling real-world datasets. Experience with LLMs, prompt engineering, and AI orchestration frameworks such as LangChain or LlamaIndex is highly preferred.
The role involves building intelligent AI solutions, optimizing model performance, integrating APIs, and contributing to scalable AI deployments.

Develop AI/ML solutions using Python and SQL.
