About the job
Finominal is a global financial technology company focused on empowering investors with technology, data, and research. Our insights are frequently featured in the WSJ, FT, and other leading media.

Our Mission:

The investment world is still full of fragmented tools, expensive software, hidden risks, high fees, and portfolios that look diversified but are often driven by the same underlying exposures. Finominal exists to change that.

We are building the go-to platform for professional investors to get an independent, data-driven perspective on funds and portfolios across global markets.

This backend software developer role is for someone early in their career who wants to work on real systems, learn fast, and take ownership much earlier than a typical fresher role. You will work directly with the CTO on the systems that power Finominal. 

Your work will include:

Making Finominal faster, safer, and more reliable
Improving API performance, testing, monitoring, and production stability
Finding security gaps, slow APIs, database bottlenecks, and production issues before users feel them
Building dashboards, alerts, and metrics using tools like Sentry and Grafana
Using AI to automate internal engineering workflows and improve how we build software
Working on AI-driven product features

This is not a role where you spend months doing isolated training tasks. You will work on production systems, real users, real APIs, real financial data, and real engineering problems.

What we're looking for:

Completed degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering, or a closely related technical field
0 to 2 years of experience
Strong programming ability in Python
Good understanding of backend development, APIs, databases, and basic web security
Comfortable working from our Bangalore office every day

What we will value highly:

Experience with Django, FastAPI, Flask, or similar backend frameworks
Experience working with PostgreSQL, SQL queries, APIs, Git, and Linux
Interest in performance optimization, testing, monitoring, and production reliability
Curiosity about AI tools and how they can improve engineering workflows
Interest in finance, investing, or data-heavy products
