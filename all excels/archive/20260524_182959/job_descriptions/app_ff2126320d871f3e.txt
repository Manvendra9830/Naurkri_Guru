Deepgrid Semi Pvt Ltd - AI Engineer
Location: Hyderabad

About the Role :

Deepgrid Semi is building custom semiconductor solutions for Autonomous Driving. We're looking for engineers to own AI model training, deployment, and low-level optimization on our custom silicon, with the range to contribute across the full stack when needed.
Core Responsibilities
Train and deploy ADAS perception, prediction, and planning models on Deepgrid's custom hardware
Write and optimize CUDA/custom kernels where you understand what happens at the hardware level and can squeeze performance out of it
Implement and tune model optimization techniques: quantization, pruning, distillation, operator fusion
Understand the math behind what you're optimizing like gradient flow, numerical stability, convergence behavior
Support the broader AI stack as needed: automated research pipelines, AI-powered UI, and software integration for our end-to-end solution
