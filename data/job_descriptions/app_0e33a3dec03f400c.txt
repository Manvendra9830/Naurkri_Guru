About the job
Role Description
This is a full-time, on-site Data Scientist role based in Coimbatore. The Data Scientist will be responsible for collecting, analyzing, and interpreting large datasets to uncover trends, patterns, and actionable insights. Day-to-day tasks include developing predictive models, building data visualizations, and implementing data-driven solutions to solve complex business problems. Collaboration with cross-functional teams to support decision-making processes is an essential aspect of this role.

Qualifications
Proficiency in Data Science and Data Analytics to build and optimize sophisticated models.
Strong expertise in Statistics and Data Analysis for interpreting complex datasets and delivering actionable insights.
Experience in Data Visualization tools and techniques to communicate findings effectively.
Proficiency in programming languages such as Python is highly desirable.
Knowledge of machine learning frameworks and data processing systems is advantageous.
Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, or a related field.
Excellent problem-solving skills and the ability to work collaboratively in a team-oriented environment.
Strong communication skills to present data-driven recommendations to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
