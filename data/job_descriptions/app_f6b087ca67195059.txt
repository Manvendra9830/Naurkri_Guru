About The Role

This is a full-time, on-site Software Development Intern role located in Noida. The intern will work closely with the development team to design, code, test, and debug software solutions. Tasks will include collaborating on backend development, writing efficient programming code, assisting with software testing and troubleshooting, and contributing to feature enhancements. The role provides an exceptional opportunity to gain hands-on technical experience in a fast-paced, career-focused environment.

Qualifications

Strong foundational knowledge in Computer Science and Software Development principles
Experience or familiarity with Back-End Web Development and related technologies
Proficiency in Programming and understanding of Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)
Ability to work collaboratively in team environments and adapt to project requirements
Eagerness to learn, problem-solving mindset, and attention to detail
Pursuing or recently completed a degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field
Previous internship or project experience in software development is a plus

Responsibilities

The Software Development Intern will be responsible for supporting the development team in various technical tasks, including but not limited to designing and implementing backend functionalities, writing clean and efficient code, conducting testing and debugging of software modules, and assisting in the integration of new features. The intern will participate in code reviews, contribute to documentation, and collaborate with team members to ensure project objectives are met within stipulated timelines. Additionally, the role involves staying updated with the latest industry trends and applying best practices to improve software quality and performance.

Benefits

Interns will receive a monthly stipend of 20,000 to 25,000 INR for a duration of 6 months. During this period, they will gain valuable industry experience, mentorship from seasoned professionals, and exposure to real-world projects. Exceptional performers may be considered for a Pre-Placement Offer (PPO) with a salary package of up to 6-9 LPA, based on performance and project contributions. The internship provides a platform for skill development, professional growth, and networking opportunities within a dynamic tech environment. Additionally, interns will have access to Meritshot's extensive industry connections and resources aimed at accelerating their career trajectory.
