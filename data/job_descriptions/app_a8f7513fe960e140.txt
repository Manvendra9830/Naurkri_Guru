About the job
Job Type & Work Mode:
• Work Mode: Flexible work modes are supported.
• Employment Type: Full-time/Part-time
• Eligibillity: 0-5 years of experience

Neso Academy is seeking passionate and experienced Computer Science Engineering Teacher to join its growing team! In this role, you'll play a key role in developing and delivering engaging educational content for our online courses.

Responsibilities:
Design and deliver engaging recorded and live lectures focused on Theoretical Computer Science, Programming Languages, and emerging AI Technologies.
Explain core computer science fundamentals clearly and progressively build towards advanced concepts, ensuring strong conceptual understanding among learners.
Teach coding concepts using programming languages.
Develop quality teaching materials, including presentations and other visual aids, to enhance the learning experience.

Requirements:
• A Bachelor’s degree (B.Tech/B.E.), and final-year B.Tech/B.E. students are also eligible to apply.
• Strong understanding of core computer science fundamentals.
• Ability to explain complex technical topics clearly, concisely, and effectively to learners at varying levels.

Extra points for candidates who have prepared/qualified for the GATE exam.
