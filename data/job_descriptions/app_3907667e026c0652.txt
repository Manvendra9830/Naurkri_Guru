About the job
Job Title: AI & Data Analytics Executive
Department: Technology and Innovation
Location: Gurgaon or Bengaluru 
Reporting To: Manager / Head of Department

Purpose/About the Role (Role Summary)
We are looking for a highly motivated and tech-savvy professional to support our transition from manual data handling to AI-powered workflows. The ideal candidate should be passionate about data analytics, automation, AI technologies, and modern application development.
This role focuses on improving operational efficiency through data-driven insights, workflow automation, AI integration, and development of scalable digital solutions using modern tools and technologies.

Primary Responsibilities

Data Analytics
Analyze structured and unstructured datasets to identify trends, patterns, and business insights.
Create interactive dashboards and reports using Power BI or similar tools.
Support management with data-backed decision-making and performance tracking.

AI Integration & Automation
Research, experiment, and implement AI solutions including Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI automation tools.
Develop AI-assisted workflows to solve internal operational and technical challenges.
Automate repetitive business and reporting tasks using Python or low-code/no-code platforms.
Integrate APIs and AI services such as OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, or similar platforms.

Full Stack Development
Develop and maintain internal web applications, dashboards, and automation portals.
Work on frontend and backend technologies for enterprise applications.
Build responsive user interfaces and integrate them with APIs and databases.
Support deployment, testing, maintenance, and performance optimization of applications.

Research & Innovation
Explore and evaluate new software tools, AI plugins, and productivity platforms.
Stay updated with the latest AI, analytics, automation, and software development trends.
Recommend innovative solutions to improve operational efficiency and digital transformation initiatives.
