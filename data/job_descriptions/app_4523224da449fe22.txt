Role Description
We are looking for an Applied AI / ML Engineer to build intelligent orchestration systems powering next-generation AI agents.
This role focuses on semantic intent routing, LLM workflows, and agentic AI infrastructure rather than traditional model research.
You will work on:
AI agent orchestration
semantic routing systems
tool selection pipelines
LangGraph workflows
structured reasoning systems
lightweight LLM optimization
production AI execution architectures
The ideal candidate understands how to transform fuzzy human requests into reliable, structured execution pipelines while optimizing latency, accuracy, and cost.
You will collaborate closely with backend and frontend engineering teams to build scalable AI systems for enterprise automation.
