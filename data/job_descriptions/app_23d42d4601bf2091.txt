About the job
Location: In-office
Company: OneNDF
Industry: Fintech / Lending / Property-Backed Loans
About OneNDF
OneNDF is a fintech platform focused on property-backed loans, helping individuals and businesses access the right financing solutions through a faster, smarter, and more guided process. We are building technology-led workflows to improve lead qualification, customer engagement, and loan conversion journeys.
Role Overview
We are looking for an AI Voice Agent Engineer / Conversational AI Developer who can build an AI calling agent from scratch for both inbound and outbound leads.
This role requires someone who can combine AI, voice workflows, automation, telephony, CRM integrations, and conversation design to create a reliable calling system that can speak with leads, qualify them, capture responses, route them correctly, and continuously improve performance.
The ideal candidate should be able to take ownership from strategy to execution — from designing the call logic and agent prompts to integrating APIs, deploying the system, and optimizing it based on real call outcomes.
Key Responsibilities
Build and deploy an AI-powered voice calling agent for inbound and outbound lead interactions
Design end-to-end conversation journeys for different loan products such as home loans, LAP, business loans, school financing, and builder-related queries
Create call flows for:
lead qualification
customer profiling
document readiness checks
follow-up reminders
appointment booking / RM assignment
missed call / callback handling
Develop the logic for multilingual customer conversations, especially across Indian borrowers
Integrate the AI agent with telephony systems, CRM, lead management tools, and internal workflows
Build prompt architecture, intent mapping, fallback responses, escalation flows, and human handover logic
Ensure the agent captures and structures customer responses accurately
Set up dashboards, transcripts, disposition tagging, and performance analytics
Improve the agent continuously based on call recordings, drop-offs, qualification quality, and conversion data
Work closely with business, sales, ops, and tech teams to align the AI agent with real customer journeys
Ensure data privacy, compliance, and appropriate safeguards in customer interactions
What the Person Will Build

Outbound AI follow-up calls for warm and cold leads
AI-based lead screening before RM allocation
Basic multilingual voice interactions
Call summaries and disposition reports

Integrations with OneNDF’s internal systems and workflows
