About the job
OneNDF is a digital platform helping MSMEs and business owners access the right debt financing solutions through a network of 120+ lenders.
We are building the next layer of lending automation — where AI voice agents can speak to customers, qualify leads, capture information, trigger WhatsApp follow-ups, and update CRM workflows intelligently.
We are looking for a hands-on builder who can work at the intersection of AI agents, voice technology, backend development, and CRM systems.
Role: AI Agent & Django CRM Developer
This is an exciting opportunity for someone who wants to build real products from scratch — not just maintain code.
You will help OneNDF build AI voice agents that can interact with customers and an internal CRM system using Django that helps our sales, telecalling, and relationship management teams track every lead from first call to loan disbursement.
What You’ll Build
You will work on building systems such as:
AI voice agents for inbound and outbound calls
Conversation flows for loan lead qualification
Customer intent detection and response capture
Call summaries and lead scoring
WhatsApp follow-up automation after calls
CRM dashboards for telecallers, team leaders, RMs, and admins
Lead assignment and follow-up workflows
Document collection tracking
Campaign-wise lead management
Partner and referral pipeline tracking
MIS and reporting dashboards
Key Responsibilities
Build AI voice agents from scratch using LLMs, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and telephony APIs
Design conversation flows for products like Loan Against Property, Home Loan, Working Capital, School Financing, and Business Loans
Integrate AI agents with CRM, WhatsApp, telephony systems, and internal dashboards
Build backend modules using Python, Django, and Django REST Framework
Create database models for leads, customers, users, products, documents, call logs, and follow-ups
Develop APIs for frontend and third-party integrations
Build role-based CRM workflows for telecallers, team leaders, relationship managers, and admins
Create lead disposition flows such as hot, warm, cold, not interested, incomplete documents, and not eligible
Work on prompt engineering, call summaries, intent detection, and lead qualification logic
Continuously test, improve, and monitor AI agent performance using real business data
Skills We’re Looking For
Strong knowledge of Python
Experience or interest in Django / Django REST Framework
Understanding of APIs, databases, and backend workflows
Interest in AI agents, LLMs, voice bots, and automation
Exposure to tools such as OpenAI, Twilio, Exotel, ElevenLabs, Deepgram, Google Speech, or similar platforms is a plus
Basic understanding of frontend technologies like HTML, CSS, JavaScript, or React is a plus
Ability to think through business workflows and convert them into working systems
Good problem-solving skills and willingness to experiment
Interest in FinTech, lending, CRM, or sales automation
