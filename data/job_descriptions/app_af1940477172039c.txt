About the job
Healthcare communication is broken. At PypeAI, we are building the "Operating Layer" for healthcare voice first agents that manage the entire patient journey, from clinical triage to automated follow-ups. We are building the infrastructure for autonomous healthcare coordination.
As an AI Engineer, you won't be on the sidelines; you’ll be at the core of our engine. You will work directly on our Voice-to-Voice (V2V) pipeline, pushing the boundaries of what’s possible in real-time, empathetic, and clinically reliable AI.
Fine-tuning Models: Optimize domain-specific LLMs and Audio Models for medical accuracy and human-like prosody.
V2V Pipeline: Engineer the cutting edge of STT (Speech-to-Text) and TTS (Text-to-Speech) to minimize latency to sub-second levels.
Inference at Scale: Deploy and optimize models to handle thousands of concurrent, high-stakes medical conversations.
Edge-of-the-Map Engineering: Work on the entire pipeline—from data synthesis to production-grade inference.
