About the job

Diagnose and resolve issues related to IDE configuration, plugin connectivity, and enterprise authentication. User Onboarding & Enablement: Assist in onboarding project teams to the GenAI ecosystem. Provide hands-on demonstrations and walkthroughs to help developers integrate these tools into their local development environments. Best Practice Guidance: Guide users on the effective use of "Prompt Engineering" within their IDEs to ensure high-quality code generation that aligns with Infosys coding and security standards. Documentation & Knowledge Management: Document frequently asked questions (FAQs), create "How-To" guides, and maintain a technical knowledge base to help users self-serve common issues. Solution Validation: Assist senior consultants in testing new features or updates within GenAI tools to ensure they are fit for purpose before being rolled out to larger teams. Feedback Integration: Proactively collect user feedback on tool performance, latency, or feature gaps. Collate this data to provide inputs for the solution roadmap and vendor discussions. Deployment Assistance: Support the rollout of tool licenses and updates, ensuring all environment dependencies are met and that developers have the necessary access permissions. Case Study Contribution: Help in identifying successful use cases where GenAI tools significantly improved productivity and assist in documenting these as internal case studies or PoVs. Required Skills: Tool Proficiency: Hands-on experience or a strong technical understanding of GenAI-powered IDEs and agents
