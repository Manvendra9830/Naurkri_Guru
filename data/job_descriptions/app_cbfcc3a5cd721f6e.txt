About the job
Company Description
Ratna Sagar, established in 1982, is a leading publisher of educational books designed to uphold the highest standards of teaching methodology, design, and pedagogical excellence. Inspired by the ancient Nalanda University’s renowned library, Ratna Sagar seeks to shape young minds through modern and proven approaches to learning. The organization is committed to delivering innovative and effective resources to educators and students across various disciplines. With a reputation for excellence, Ratna Sagar has become synonymous with quality educational publishing.

Job Description: 
Are you a passionate and creative individual who is eager to learn every day? Do you believe in the power of content to inspire and educate? If yes, then, you could be the one we are looking for. We are seeking enthusiastic individuals for an editorial role who may not have extensive editorial experience but are driven by a purpose—those who are eager to grow, innovate, and make a meaningful impact through their work.

Required skills and qualifications: 
Curiosity and a Strong Desire to Learn: We welcome candidates who are naturally inquisitive and open to exploring new ideas, techniques, and concepts in content creation. Your willingness to develop your skills will be essential in this role.
Creativity and Adaptability: In our dynamic environment, the ability to think outside the box is crucial. We are looking for individuals who can embrace change and adjust their approach to meet evolving content needs.
Purpose beyond Income: We seek candidates who are passionate about the editorial profession for reasons that extend beyond financial gain. A genuine love for storytelling and a desire to make a difference are key qualities we admire.
No Formal Editorial Experience Required: While formal experience is not necessary, enthusiasm for learning and a commitment to personal and professional growth are essential. Freshers and experienced individuals alike are welcome to apply!

 Role Responsibilities: 
Collaborate with team members to brainstorm and develop engaging content ideas.
Assist in researching topics and fact-checking to ensure accuracy and relevance.
Edit and proofread content, providing constructive feedback to enhance quality.
Help maintain our editorial standards and ensure alignment with our brand voice.
