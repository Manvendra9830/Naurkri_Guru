About the job
🚀 Hiring: Agentic AI Developer | Founding-Level Role 🏢 Hindustan Drone Services Limited (HDSL) 📍 Hyderabad, India (Hybrid Flexibility Available) 💼 Full-Time | AI-Native Startup ---
🌐 About HDSL & Our Vision
HDSL is building India’s next-generation Drone-as-a-Service (DaaS) platform—an AI-native marketplace connecting enterprises, drone operators, and consumers across all sectors.
We operate differently. HDSL follows a strict MVi Model:
Micro-team
Virtual infrastructure / Extreme automation
intelligent, AI-native execution
Instead of scaling through massive operational teams, we scale through an intelligent AI workforce. We are replacing legacy manual workflows with autonomous AI agents across support, sales, dispatch, QA, and vertical intelligence. This role sits at the absolute center of that vision.
🛠️ What You’ll Build (The Agent Ecosystem)
You won’t be building generic demo chatbots. You will architect, deploy, and manage production-grade, autonomous multi-agent systems:
AI Marketing, Customer Support & Sales Agents: AI Marketing thru plethora of channels, 24/7 multilingual voice + chat support, alongside personalized B2B prospecting and pipeline automation.
AI Operator Dispatch Agent: Intelligent pilot matching, SLA routing, cluster-based dispatch, and live availability orchestration.
AI Coding, QA & DevOps Agents: Self-healing code workflows, automated testing (Playwright + LLMs), and deployment validation pipelines.
Vertical Intelligence Agents: Processing deep industry data (Crop intelligence/NDVI analysis, construction mapping, solar inspection, and surveillance).
🎯 Core Responsibilities
Agent Architecture & Orchestration: Build robust multi-agent systems with advanced reasoning pipelines, state management, tool usage, fallback handling, and telemetry.
AI Infrastructure & RAG: Implement advanced retrieval pipelines, long-term contextual memory, vector databases, and domain-aware knowledge graphs.
Systems Integration: Seamlessly connect autonomous agents with our core Python/FastAPI backend, Flutter mobile apps, and AWS cloud infrastructure.
Evaluation & Observability: Design strict evaluation frameworks to monitor agent latency, cost, reliability, and token efficiency while aggressively eliminating hallucinations.
🧰 The Tech Stack
AI & Agentic Frameworks: Claude API, OpenAI APIs, Gemini, LangGraph, LangChain, LlamaIndex, Custom Function Calling
Backend & DB: Python, FastAPI (Async architectures), PostgreSQL + pgvector, Pinecone / Weaviate
Infrastructure & CI/CD: AWS (Lambda, ECS/Fargate, S3, CloudWatch), Docker, GitHub Actions
Integrations: Retell AI, ElevenLabs, Clay, DroneDeploy, Agromonitoring, AWS Rekognition
