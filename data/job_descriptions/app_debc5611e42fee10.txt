About the job
Opportunity
As an AI Product Prototype Engineer, your primary goal is to rapidly convert product ideas, customer problems, and internal requirements into working prototypes for web apps, mobile apps, WhatsApp flows, AI bots, and internal tools.
You will work closely with the founder and product team to understand business problems, design practical user flows, build functional prototypes using AI-assisted coding tools, and quickly iterate based on feedback from customers, management, and internal teams.
This is not a traditional software engineering role focused on production-grade code. It is also not a pure UI/UX design role focused only on screens. This role sits between product thinking, UX, rapid coding, and experimentation.
You will be expected to build fast, test fast, learn fast, and improve fast. The final validated prototype will then be handed over to the engineering team to build into a scalable, secure, production-ready application.
This role requires curiosity, speed, product sense, basic coding ability, and comfort with modern AI tools. You should be able to take an unclear idea, break it into a simple workflow, build a usable prototype, show it to users, capture feedback, and improve it without waiting for perfect instructions.
Beyond building prototypes, you are expected to document product logic, user flows, customer feedback, edge cases, and handover notes so that the engineering team can clearly understand what needs to be built.

Responsibilities
You convert product ideas, customer problems, and internal requirements into working prototypes for web apps, mobile apps, WhatsApp flows, AI bots, BotFlow journeys, and internal tools.
You apply AI-first thinking to every product idea, identifying where AI, automation, agents, bots, recommendations, decisioning, or intelligent workflows can create a better customer experience and reduce manual effort.
You work closely with the Founder and Product Team to understand the problem, clarify the use case, define the user flow, and quickly build a functional version that can be tested with customers or internal teams.
You design workflows by first asking how AI can simplify, automate, personalize, predict, recommend, or improve the user journey instead of simply digitizing the existing manual process.
You write code and build functional prototypes across frontend, mobile, automation flows, chatbot flows, APIs, and simple backend/database logic wherever required.
You build prototypes for customer-facing applications, mobile apps, web apps, WhatsApp journeys, AI bots, BotFlow experiences, and internal operational tools.
You use modern AI coding tools, product tools, automation platforms, and prototyping frameworks to build fast, usable, and testable prototypes.
You build prototypes that are good enough to test the idea, collect feedback, and validate the workflow, but not necessarily production-grade.
You participate in customer feedback calls with the Founder or Product Manager, understand customer pain points, observe how customers react to the prototype, and capture improvement areas clearly.
You iterate quickly on prototypes based on feedback from customers, management, product, support, and engineering teams.
You document the product logic, user flow, assumptions, edge cases, customer feedback, and final prototype behavior in a structured format for the engineering team.
You prepare clear handover notes for engineering so the team can convert the validated prototype into a secure, scalable, production-grade product.
Success in this role is measured by speed of prototype development, quality of AI-first product thinking, usefulness of customer feedback, number of validated ideas, and smooth handover to the engineering team.
